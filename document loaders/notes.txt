Agentic AI is an advanced approach to artificial intelligence in which AI systems can plan tasks, use tools, remember relevant information, and take actions to achieve a goal. Unlike a basic chatbot that mainly responds to questions, an AI agent can complete multi-step activities, check results, and adjust its actions when needed. Multiple agents can also work together by sharing tasks and information. Agentic AI has applications in software development, research, education, customer support, and business automation. However, it also faces challenges such as incorrect decisions, security risks, privacy concerns, high costs, and the need for human supervision. Future research aims to make AI agents more reliable, transparent, efficient, and safe.
